# Crossy Road (com_yodo1_crossyroad)
Source: hand-drawn SVG (flat isometric polygons) from the Play Store icon and the exported fg layer
(/home/claude/refs/com_yodo1_crossyroad/play.png). No ready-made logo exists for the chicken (only a Lawnicons line drawing).
Design: the whole white chicken (R12, R15), no sky/background. White top, near-white front face, lavender right face (#EAE4F6 to #D6D8F2),
red comb (#F5304F / #FF6B8A), red wattle, orange-yellow beak (#FFCB6B / #FF9A48), dark eye, orange feet, small tail and wing blocks.
Differs from the Play icon: it shows the full chicken (feet, tail, wing) so it fits the icon, instead of a cropped close-up.
Version 4 wrongly tinted the body blue; fixed. The front face is a hair off-white (#F4F4FB) so the blocks stay readable; Arch can ask for pure white.
