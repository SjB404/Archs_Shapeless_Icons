# My Little Pony (Gameloft) - Twilight Sparkle's head

Source: Play Store icon (512px) `/home/claude/refs/com_gameloft_android_anmp_gloftpohm/play.png`, the game's own art.
No ready-made logo exists (the library file is only the Gameloft badge, which is dropped).

What was done (R15, R5):
- Cut the pony out of the cyan background and white sticker outline; removed the My Little Pony title logo, the Gameloft badge, the wing and the crown. Cut under the chin at the neck.
- The Play art crops the horn tip, the top of the mane and the right side of the mane, and the title logo hid the back of the ear/head. Those parts were redrawn to match (horn tip, rounded mane top and side, ear/neck edge).
- Traced with vtracer (flat-colour art traces cleanly) and clipped to the cut-out outline. SVG, no embedded bitmap.

Differences from the app icon: no background, no round crop, no logo, no badge; head only, as big as possible.
Look at: the mane is dark navy, so on the dark drawer it relies on its thin darker rim and the pink/purple streaks. The neck ends in a flat cut.
