# Steam

- Source: Valve's own vector logo (store.steampowered.com header, logo_steam.svg); gradient colours taken from the Play Store icon (navy #091b40 at the top to Steam blue #1389b9 at the bottom).
- Decision (R8, Arch's words): Steam stays a full circle with the white piston inside, filling the icon. Colours are the real ones (R13), no extra outline.
- Checked the earlier draft: shapes and gradient match the store icon, so it was kept as it was. The rod's end is pure white and the ring around the big head stays see-through to the blue.
- Differs from the exported original: the original's gradient was flipped (bright on top); this follows the Play Store icon (navy top, bright blue bottom).
- Doubt: the navy top edge is dark against the dark grey drawer, but still reads clearly as a circle.
