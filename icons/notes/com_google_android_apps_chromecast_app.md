# Google Home (version 7)
Arch (M10): "make the Google home corners as rounded as the Google store (in terms of optical roundness, not software
rounding values cus a sharp and weak corner with the same rounding on each look like different rounding".

Redrawn as clean geometry from the dashboard-icons google-home drawing (walls 8.5 to 503.5, roof at 45 degrees, even
thickness 110, inside see-through), with Google's colours. Corners: the Play Store's 60-degree corners have a curve of
about 53 px at icon size; every Home corner gets the same curve length: right angles (peak, bottom corners) radius 69,
the wide eaves radius 138. Compared side by side with the Play Store before choosing.
