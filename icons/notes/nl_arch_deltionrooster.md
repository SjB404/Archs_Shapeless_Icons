New in the export of 19 Nov 2026. Arch's own app (syncs his Deltion class schedule into his calendar).

Its icon is a white calendar on Deltion orange (#F58220, the icon's own background colour). Redrawn as clean shapes
measured from the export (body 44 x 40 dp with 4 dp corners, two 4 dp tabs, 8 dp date squares). R9: the white logo
takes the background colour, so the calendar is orange; the five date squares, which showed the orange background,
are see-through.
