# Creative studio

Added in version 9 from the Samsung export of 21 Nov 2026 (M14).

Creative studio: the white easel with the blue-green scribble and blue legs, from the app's logo layer; blue background left out (R5). The board is white in the app, so it stays white (R12).

Version 9.1 (M16, M17): flattened. Creative studio: the white easel with the scribble, from the app's logo layer. Flat white easel without its soft shading (M16); the scribble's blue-to-teal fade is part of the drawing, so it keeps a simple two-colour fade. The board is white in the app, so it stays white (R12).

Version 9.2 (M18, Arch's marked-up screenshots of version 9.1: the end of the scribble was smeared (circled)). Redrawn: Creative studio: the easel board with its hook, lavender bottom ledge and two legs, and the scribble, outline traced from the 512 px Galaxy Store icon (M17). The board is white in the app, so it stays white (R12); hook, ledge and legs keep their light lavender. The scribble's colours run purple, blue, teal, green along the stroke as in the app, so it keeps that fade, without the 3D shading (M16).
