# Lekker Onderweg (version 8)
New in the export of 19 Nov 2026. NS's app with digital savings cards for the station shops (Kiosk, Julia's,
Luca's by Julia's, StationsHuiskamer).

Its icon: a white paper cup and a croissant on purple, with a long shadow. Kept the cup and the croissant:
- Cup: redrawn as clean shapes measured from the export (tapered body, white lip, lavender #AAABD6 opening). A paper
  cup really is white, so it stays white (R12).
- Croissant: the five pieces traced from the export and smoothed, golden #E28D02 tops and #B27A4A lower parts (the
  icon's own two tones, flat).
- The purple background and long shadow are left out. The purple outlines between the croissant pieces, and between
  the croissant and the cup, became see-through gaps.
