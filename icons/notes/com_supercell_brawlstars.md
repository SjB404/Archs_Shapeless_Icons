# Brawl Stars (com_supercell_brawlstars)

Arch: "brawl stars should just be the brawl skull" (R15, R12).

- Source: Play Store icon `/home/claude/refs/com_supercell_brawlstars/play.png` (no official skull SVG exists;
  the Lawnicons drawing is only a line outline, Simple Icons only has the Supercell word).
- Method: cut the white skull out of the Play icon (dark ring, glow and lightning dropped), traced its outline and
  its feature holes, and smoothed them into curves. Saved as vector paths, no embedded bitmap.
- Look: white skull (stays white, R12) with near-black eyes, brows and nose triangle (#0b1a12, the dark green-black of the original).
- Differs from the app icon: no ring, no blue burst, no drop shadow under the jaw.
- Check: the jaw corners are slightly rounder than the original after smoothing.
