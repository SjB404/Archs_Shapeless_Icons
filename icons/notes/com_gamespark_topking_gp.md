# Top Lords (com_gamespark_topking_gp)

Source: the Play Store icon (`/home/claude/refs/com_gamespark_topking_gp/play.png`, 512 px). No official
standalone king art or library logo exists for this game.

What I did (R15, main subject only):
- Kept only the king (gold crown, blue cape, arm and crossbow). Removed the red soldiers, the blue gate,
  the sword/shield sign, the "+1" sign, the floor and the round crop.
- Cut him out by flood-filling the background up to the art's own dark outline, enlarged 3x, cleaned the edge
  (2 px eroded, soft edge) so no halo from the old background is left. The see-through gap inside the crossbow is transparent.
- The "+1" sign was left out: it sits behind the king and can't be kept without the gate.

Why a PNG and not an SVG: I traced it with vtracer (both modes). The result flattened the cape folds and
shading, made the outline jagged and the crossbow muddy. The cut-out looks clearly better. File is
952x904 px, transparent background.

Things to know:
- The cape's bottom edge is a straight cut, as in the original icon (the art continues past the icon edge).
- The king's thin dark-navy outline is part of the art; it's kept. It's slightly less visible on the dark drawer.
- The source is a 512 px picture, so at huge sizes it is a little soft; fine at phone size.
