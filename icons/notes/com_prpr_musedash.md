# MuseDash (com_prpr_musedash)

Source: Play Store icon (512 px), `refs/com_prpr_musedash/play.png`, delivered as `icons/src/com_prpr_musedash.png`
(.png, not a traced SVG: it is detailed painted art; tracing would lose the eyes and shading).

- The icon is a close-up of the girl's face with her hair filling the whole picture. There is no
  background behind her to remove: the corners are her hair. So the cut-out has nothing to cut,
  and it ends up as the full square (no round crop, no launcher shape).
- Looked for full official art: the Muse Dash wiki (musedash.fandom.com) has full-body art of Rin, Buro
  and Marija, but none of them is this girl, and no uncropped version of this icon art turned up
  (App Store uses the same crop).
- Removed the tiny transparency the Play file had (edges were 90-100% opaque).

For Arch: on the phone this reads as a pink square tile. If you'd rather have a head-shaped
silhouette, we need the full uncropped illustration (not found); I did not invent an outline.
