# bol (com_bol_shop)

- Source: no ready-made current "bol." SVG was available (Wikimedia's 2019 file is the old "bol.com" logo; the 2025 file download was refused, not retried; Lawnicons is only a thin line sketch). Traced the real current logo from the Play Store icon (/home/claude/refs/com_bol_shop/play.png, white "bol." on #0000A4).
- Did: upscaled, traced b, o, l with vtracer (binary, spline), and redrew the dot as a clean circle, because the Play icon crops it at the right edge (dot measured: diameter equal to the letter stem-to-baseline 66 px of 512, sitting on the baseline).
- Colour: #7F9CFF, a lighter bol blue (real brand blue #0000A4 vanishes on the dark drawer). Readable on both #2F2F2F and #4F7D95 and at 48 px. Dot same colour. Wordmark only, no container (R14, R10, R24).
- Look at: letter shapes are traced, so the curves are very close to the real logo but not the official vector.
