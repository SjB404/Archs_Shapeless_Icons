# Angry Crusher Catapult (com_stellarstudiogames_abcandybirds)

- Source: the installed icon's own art (export `fg.png`, 432px), redrawn by hand as a vector SVG. No official logo exists.
- Design (R15): yellow bird, rubber bands, pouch and wooden slingshot, cut out; no sky, clouds, grass, ground or round crop.
- The slingshot trunk ends flat where the ground was.
- The Play Store icon is a different, newer picture (an orange fruit); I followed the icon installed on Arch's phone (yellow bird), as the direction says.
- Colours taken from the installed art: yellow #F5C824, orange beak #F7941D, wood #C98F5E, bands/wraps browns.
- Doubt: the wide layout (bird plus slingshot) makes the bird fairly small at 48px.
