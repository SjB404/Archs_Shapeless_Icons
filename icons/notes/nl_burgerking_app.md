# Burger King NL (`nl_burgerking_app`)

**Now:** the real Burger King logo: two orange bun halves with the red "BURGER KING" letters between
them. Nothing behind it, sharp edges, and the holes in the B, R and G are see-through.

## What Arch said
- Version 2: "Burger king has cutout residue"
- Version 4: "the burger king logo still looks messy"

Version 4 had been cut out of a picture. It had cream blobs stuck in the holes of the B and R,
dark fuzzy edges and white specks between the letters. This version is a clean drawing, not a cut-out.

## Sources
- Shapes: the official logo drawing on Wikimedia Commons, `File:Burger_King_2020.svg`
  (https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Burger_King_2020.svg, file:
  https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/c/cc/Burger_King_2020.svg). Copy saved in
  `/home/claude/refs/nl_burgerking_app/dl_wikimedia/`.
- Colours: the official Burger King colours, "Flaming Orange" #FF8732 (buns) and "Fiery Red"
  #D62300 (letters). These are exactly the colours in the app's own icon on Arch's phone (`fg.png`).
  Brand palette pages say the same: https://madegooddesigns.com/burger-king-brand-guidelines/,
  https://brandpalettes.com/burger-king-color-codes/

## What I did
- Took the official drawing's buns and letters as they are. Removed its cream (#F5EBDC) burger-shaped
  backing, which is the background colour of the app icon (rules R5 and R7).
- Checked the shapes against the app's own icon layer, pixel by pixel: they match except for edge
  smoothing. The Play Store picture is about 1% wider, but the app's own icon and the official
  drawing agree, so I kept the official proportions (R6: no stretching).
- Words stay, because here the words and the bun together are the logo (R14).

## Different from the app's real icon
- No cream circle or square behind it.
- The tiny ® after "KING" is left out: it's too small to see on the phone and isn't part of what
  people recognise (version 4 didn't have it either).

## For Arch to look at
- The gap between "BURGER" and "KING" is thin, as in the real logo, so at the smallest size the
  two lines sit very close together. They still read fine in the preview.
- On the blue-grey wallpaper, the red letters stand out mainly by colour (red against blue), since
  they're about as bright as the wallpaper. They're easy to read in the preview, and Burger King
  isn't one of your home screen apps.
