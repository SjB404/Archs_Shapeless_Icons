# fomo (family_fomo_app)

Source: traced from the Play Store icon (/home/claude/refs/family_fomo_app/play.png, the app on Arch's phone,
NOT the "fomo" in the logo libraries, which is a different company). Colours from that icon:
eyes #E8EBFF, pupils #0B091F (the icon's own dark navy).

What I did: traced the two light eyes, removed the dark square background, drew both pupils as filled navy
slanted shapes. In the original the left pupil is open to the edge (a notch), which is what read as "missing a pupil".
I closed the left eye with a thin light rim and filled the pupil, same as the right one.

Check: left pupil's rim thickness is my choice (thin), the real logo has no rim there.
