# Gmail (version 7)
Arch (M10): "gmail should have the same shape, but have a white background rectangle and be dualtone red".
Then: "make the gmail logo a full rectangle, the m is sticking out of the logo".

The 2020 M from dashboard-icons (gmail.svg) in two reds: each side (rounded top and stem as one shape, so no seam)
dark red #C5221F, the V Google red #EA4335 (the same two reds as the 2020 logo's top-left corner and V).
Behind it a full white envelope rectangle: flat top at the highest point of the M's rounded corners, its top corners
follow the M's own rounding, bottom corners as the M's. So the outline is one clean rounded rectangle and the M no
longer sticks out above the white.
