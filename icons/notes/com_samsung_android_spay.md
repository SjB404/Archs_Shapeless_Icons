# Wallet

Added in version 9 from the Samsung export of 21 Nov 2026 (M14).

Samsung Wallet: the white wallet card with the coloured cards and the diagonal flap from the app's logo layer; blue background left out (R5). The card is white in the app, so it stays white (R12).

Version 9.1 (M16, M17): flattened. Samsung Wallet: the white wallet card with the green, blue and purple cards and the white flap across them, outline traced from the Play Store icon (512 px) (M17). Flat colours, the frosted shading dropped (M16); the card is white in the app, so it stays white (R12).

Version 9.2 (M18, Arch's marked-up screenshots of version 9.1: the flap's slanted edge didn't show (arrow)). Redrawn: Samsung Wallet: the wallet with its three cards (green, blue, purple) and the white flap with its slanted edge, outline traced from the 512 px Galaxy Store icon (M17); the cards are measured rounded rectangles. The wallet is white in the app, so it stays white (R12): the flap white, the frame around the cards light lavender as in the app, so the flap's edge shows. Flat (M16).
