# Balatro (com_playstack_balatro_android)

Source: `icons/src/com_playstack_balatro_android.pixel.png`, 43x61 px, native size, straight, hard edges, transparent.

- Taken from the unrotated Joker card sprite on the Balatro wiki: https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/balatrogame/images/e/ef/Joker.png/revision/latest?cb=20250619163211 (page: https://balatrogame.fandom.com/wiki/File:Joker.png). Saved in `refs/com_playstack_balatro_android/dl/`.
- The wiki file is the 71x95 card drawn at exactly 2x; I checked every art pixel is a clean 2x2 block and reduced it to 1x, so one image pixel = one art pixel. No smoothing, no rotation.
- Removed: card white, border, the "JOKER" lettering, stray specks. Kept: the clown's own dark outline and the white face/pompoms.
- Differs from the store icon: no cards, no hearts card, not tilted, much bigger.
- Check: the clown's outline is dark grey-blue (#4F6367), as in the game; it is faint on the dark drawer but the clown still reads clearly.
