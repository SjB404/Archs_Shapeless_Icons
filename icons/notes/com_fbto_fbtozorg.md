# FBTO Zorg app (com_fbto_fbtozorg)

- Logo: the "FBTO" word only (Arch: "FBTO should be the word only"). No white pill, no medical case.
- Source: no official SVG found (Wikimedia Commons has none, not in the libraries). Hand-drawn in SVG,
  measured from the Play Store icon (/home/claude/refs/com_fbto_fbtozorg/play.png).
- Colours: F, B, T white (real colour is dark blue #004C9C, which vanishes on the dark drawer, R10).
  The O keeps FBTO's real multicolour ring (pink, red, orange, yellow, green, cyan, blue, violet),
  built from 90 small wedges whose colours were sampled from the Play icon.
- The ring's gradient is an approximation of the soft watercolour-like original.
