# GIF Maker-Editor
Hand-drawn round badge (R8): silver ring, black disc with red glow, pale top highlights, 'GIF' drawn as shapes (no font) in the rainbow orange/yellow/green/cyan/white gradient like v4. The Play Store icon has plain white letters; v4 and the export show rainbow letters, so rainbow was kept (per the direction). Rainbow background removed; v4's orange specks at the edge are gone.
