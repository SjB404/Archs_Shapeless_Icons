# The Everything Key

Added in version 10.7 from the export of 24 Nov 2026, 09:11 (M23: "make everything key icon").

- Source: the 512 px Play Store icon (https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.spandone.everythingkey),
  the same design as the icon on Arch's phone.
- Measured: the hexagon's six edges are fitted lines (flat top and bottom, near-regular, about 205 from centre to
  corner) with evenly rounded corners, radius 68 (per-corner fits scattered from 59 to 75 with no pattern, so noise);
  the six dashes are equal bars with round ends (30.2 wide, 71.8 long) at 0, 60, 120, 180, 240 and 300 degrees around
  their centre; the centre dot is a circle of radius 32.3. Overlay with the source: 99.4% the same shape.
- Colours: grey #D1D2D2, black #1B1D1F, red #D71921 (top-left and bottom-right dashes).
- The hexagon is the logo itself, so it stays (R8); the white circle behind it is left out (R5).
