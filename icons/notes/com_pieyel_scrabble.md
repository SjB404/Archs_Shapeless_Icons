# Scrabble GO (com_pieyel_scrabble)

- Source: hand-built SVG. "S" and "1" are Inter Bold glyphs converted to paths (no font needed). No ready-made logo fits (only a Lawnicons line drawing of a generic tile).
- Design: the letter tile as a rounded square (the tile is the logo, R8/R15), orange gradient (#FFB52E to #F58A14) with white S and subscript 1, as on Arch's phone. No pumpkin/Halloween scene, no circle crop.
- Differs from the phone icon: that one is a seasonal Halloween (pumpkin) version cropped round; the Play Store icon is the normal cream tile with a dark S. I kept the orange from Arch's version so it reads on dark and matches v4.
- Doubt: the app's regular look is cream with a dark S. If Arch prefers that, swap colours (cream tile #FFF1CE, dark S #2B2224).
