## What it is
Arch's sketch, redrawn as a small cartoon delivery van: side view, facing right, yellow body, grey cab window at the
top right where the front slopes down, big red "DHL" across the side, two dark wheels. The letters are the real DHL
letter shapes (from Simple Icons, "dhl").

## What was added beyond the sketch, and why
- A proper van outline: flat roof, sloped windscreen and a short rounded nose, so it reads as a van and not a box.
- Three short yellow dashes behind the van, so it looks like it's driving fast. They stand in for the speed lines
  that sit next to the letters in the real DHL logo.
- A red strip along the bottom that curves up over each wheel (wheel arches and bumper in one). It brings in more
  DHL red and frames the dark tyres.
- A pale headlight at the front and a faint door line behind the window, for a bit of character. The door line is
  only visible when the icon is big; at phone size it disappears without hurting anything.
- Wheels with a light grey hub and a dark centre, so the tyres still show on the dark grey drawer.

## Colours
- DHL yellow #FFCC00 (body, dashes), DHL red #D40511 (letters, bottom strip)
- Window grey #A3AEB6, headlight cream #FFF6CF, door line darker yellow #E3B000
- Tyres near-black #1B1B1B, hubs light grey #D5DADE

Flat colours only, no gradients, nothing behind the van.
