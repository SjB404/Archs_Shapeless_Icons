# Smart Switch

Added in version 9 from the Samsung export of 21 Nov 2026 (M14).

Smart Switch: the S made of two arrows from the app's logo layer, in the app's blue-purple gradient (R9).

Version 9.1 (M16, M17): flattened. Smart Switch: the S with its two arrowheads, outline traced from the Play Store icon (512 px) so the shape matches exactly (M17). Lavender S with white arrowheads on blue-purple in the app: flat blue-purple S with lighter arrowheads (R9, M16).

Version 9.2 (M18, Arch's marked-up screenshots of version 9.1: the lower half of the S was wrong (circled)). Redrawn: Smart Switch: the S made of two arrows, each with its arrowhead, outline traced from the 512 px Galaxy Store icon (M17). The lower arrow is white on the blue-purple tile in the app, so it takes that colour, a little lighter so it shows on dark grey (R9, R24); the upper arrow keeps its light lavender. One flat colour per arrow (M16).
