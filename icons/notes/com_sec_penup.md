# PENUP

Added in version 9 from the Samsung export of 21 Nov 2026 (M14).

PENUP: the white pen-nib arrow from the app, in the app's blue gradient (R9).

Version 9.1 (M16, M17): flattened. PENUP: the pen-nib arrow with its slit, outline traced from the Play Store icon (512 px) so the shape matches exactly (M17). White on blue in the app, so one flat blue (R9, M16).

Version 9.2 (M18, Arch's marked-up screenshots of version 9.1: the dark tip of the nib was missing (circled)). Redrawn: PENUP: the pen-nib arrow with its slit and its darker tip, outline traced from the 512 px Galaxy Store icon (M17). The white nib takes the tile's blue (R9); the tip is dark navy in the app, shown as a deeper blue so it doesn't vanish on dark grey (R10). Flat, without the 3D shading (M16).
Source: https://galaxystore.samsung.com/detail/com.sec.penup (512 px icon, saved as refs/com_sec_penup/galaxystore.png). Checked by laying the outline over the source at 2048 px (under 1% of edge pixels differ).
