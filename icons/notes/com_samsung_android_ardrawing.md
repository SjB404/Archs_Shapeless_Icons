# AR Doodle

Added in version 9 from the Samsung export of 21 Nov 2026 (M14).

AR Doodle: the rainbow squiggle from the app's logo layer, purple background left out (R5).

## Redrawn in versions 9.3 to 9.8 (M19)

Arch asked to check all v9 icons and redo the bad ones. Version 9 had traced the small export and skipped looking up the real logo; this redraw follows ICON_PROCESS steps 2 and 5.

- Source: Galaxy Store icon, 512 px (https://galaxystore.samsung.com/detail/com.samsung.android.ardrawing).
- Now: AR Doodle: the squiggle as one even line fitted to the logo, with its pink-to-cyan fade along the line and no tube shading; the yellow part crosses over the pink start.
- Checked by laying it over the source, then on dark grey and black at phone sizes.
